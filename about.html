Welcome to Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a local bakery based in Pretoria, South Africa. We specialise in freshly baked cakes, cupcakes, pastries and other delicious treats.

Our goal is to create delicious baked goods that make every celebration more special. From birthday cakes to simple afternoon treats, everything is prepared with care.

We believe that great baking starts with quality ingredients, creativity and a whole lot of love.

❤️

Our Mission
